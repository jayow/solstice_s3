What might my flares be worth?

Your flares divided by the season total, times whatever SLX ends up being allocated to Season 3. The first two numbers we measure on-chain. The third nobody knows yet — so it is an input, not an assumption.

The S3 allocation has not been announced. Solstice publishes a campaign-wide SLX base of 7.5% of a 1,000,000,000 SLX supply, but has not said how much of that lands on Season 3 specifically. Every figure below therefore moves linearly with the allocation you choose — treat it as "what if", not a forecast. S2's own allocation shifted more than once before it settled.

Linear in the allocation: double it and your SLX doubles. The share is what the dashboard measures; the allocation is what Solstice has yet to announce.

How the share is computed. Your flares come from this project's independent on-chain indexing — every quest walked from Solana state and multiplied by Solstice's published multiplier. The season total is Solstice's own published figure, so the ratio compares our measurement against their denominator and will not match their per-wallet display exactly. Exponent YT is credited only for days observed on-chain, so YT-heavy wallets read as a floor. Referral flares are not on-chain and are never included.
